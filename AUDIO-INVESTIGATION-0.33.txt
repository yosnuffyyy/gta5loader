MISSING BANKS AND DECODING
Historical logs report update:/x64/audio/sfx/RESIDENT/LOW_LATENCY.awc missing
and cutscene duration mismatches. Preserve these errors. Do not create empty
banks or mark failed loads successful. Archive-internal lookup/version/mount
problems can occur even if the host read service reports zero I/O errors.
The phone's relevant archives/PCM were not supplied for this investigation;
do not infer a decoder failure or fabricate bank contents. Determine which
requested banks supply each cue, distinguish optional resident/low-latency
content from mission speech/weapons, and instrument real mixer PCM first.
