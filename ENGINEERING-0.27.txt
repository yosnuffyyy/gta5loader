   original BC1 byte retention and changed valid-cube contents are checked.
   Six fixture runs each verify 32 draws and all 8,192 pixels: 49,152 pixels

   two reject an ambiguous/nonfinite selector with a 2D view. These expected
   rejections are assertions, not silently accepted draws.

   inactive programs across offsets and constant-buffer recreation. The final
   reported count-1/selector-2 state reproduces the safe mismatch. The report
   callback verifies the bounded history and both original constant blocks.
   This is pipeline/binding validation, not a full captured game-frame replay.
3. A fixed 16-entry diagnostic history retains changing decisions, resource
   generations, buffer offsets, raw proof values and command positions.
   Consecutive equal states coalesce. It does not participate in rendering
   or caching decisions. On fatal failure only, capture the packed CB3
   (944 bytes) and CB6 (496 bytes), matching the drawing offset/zero-fill ABI.
4. Fixed a reporting defect: ordinary bounded progress logs could discard
   fatal binding evidence during subsequent timing/cleanup output. A separate
   16,384-character capture now preserves that evidence and appends it to
   the final failure report. A sanitizer test reproduces log rollover,
   verifies both constant blocks and sampler survive, and checks run reset.
