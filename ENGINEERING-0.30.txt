CONFIRMED CAUSE AND FIX
Native0.29 set canCancelContentTouches=NO on the entire menu scroll view.
Once a content control began tracking, UIKit could not cancel it to begin
scrolling. Four new sliders also placed the folder selector further down.
NativeMenuScrollView now enables cancellation for ordinary menu content,
including buttons, sliders and switches. Only active NativeGameControls
and its descendants retain their touches until release; game multitouch
is preserved. Scroll dragging/bounce remains enabled. No gesture delegate,
private API, per-frame polling or input/graphics synchronization is added.
The folder button is immediately after the game view (hidden before launch),
above Fullscreen and the four sliders: "Select / check web folder".
