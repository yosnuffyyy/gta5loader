Signing route
GitHub Actions builds an unsigned IPA. Windows Sideloadly signs and installs
it using the user's free Apple account. Account sign-in, two-factor approval,
USB trust and Developer Mode prompts are completed locally by the user.
Free-account signing needs refreshing every seven days. No Apple credentials
or signing keys enter this repository; no JIT entitlement is required.
